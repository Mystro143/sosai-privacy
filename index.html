Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 October 2026

1. About this policy

The Sosai app is provided by Sosai Renewable Energies ("Sosai", "we", "us"). It lets our customers check their account and top up their electricity meter or solar system. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, and the choices you have.

2. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and phone number, and your meter number or system ID. Electricity-meter customers sign in with their meter number only; we create a private sign-in for that meter. Solar customers sign in with their phone number and system ID.
  • Meter and system details held by our energy partners and shown in the app, such as tariff, meter type, area, and your payment progress.
  • Payment records: the amount, date, payment reference, units purchased, and the electricity token or activation code issued to you.
  • Meter reset requests: the time and outcome of any reset-code request, so we can apply daily limits and help you if something goes wrong.
  • Basic technical logs kept by our service providers to run the service and fix errors.

Card and bank details are entered on the secure Paystack payment page. We never see or store them.

The app does not ask for access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos or location.

3. How we use your information

  • To sign you in and show your account.
  • To process your top-ups and issue your electricity token or activation code.
  • To keep payment records and receipts, and to give you support.
  • To prevent fraud and misuse, for example by limiting the number of reset codes per day.
  • To meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We do not show advertising in the app and we do not sell your personal information.

4. Who we share it with

We share information only as needed to deliver the service, with these providers:

  • Paystack to process card and bank payments.
  • Supabase to host our database and sign-in system securely.
  • Calin to generate electricity tokens for prepaid meters.
  • Angaza to activate pay-as-you-go solar systems.

We may also disclose information when the law requires it, or to protect our customers, our staff or our business from fraud or harm.

5. Legal basis for using your information

We handle personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. We rely on: performing our agreement with you (providing power and processing your payments), our legitimate interest in running and securing the service, our legal obligations, and your consent where it is required.

6. Security and how long we keep data

Data sent between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit. Access to our database is restricted, and each customer can only see their own records. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

We keep your information for as long as you are a customer and for as long afterwards as we need it to meet legal, tax and accounting requirements, or to resolve disputes.

7. Your rights and deleting your data

You can ask us to give you a copy of your information, correct it, delete it, or stop using it for a particular purpose. You can also withdraw consent where we rely on it.

To make a request, including a request to delete your account data, email safiya@sosairen.org from the address linked to your account, or tell us your meter number or system ID. We aim to respond within 30 days. Some payment records may have to be kept where the law requires it. You also have the right to complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

8. Children

The app is meant for adults. It is not directed at anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Transfers outside Nigeria

Some of our service providers may process data on servers outside Nigeria. When this happens we only use providers that protect personal data to a high standard.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed. If the changes are significant, we will tell you in the app.
